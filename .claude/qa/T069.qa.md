## Manual QA — T069

**Prerequisites:**
- The full stack is running: docker-up infra, the gateway, and the user, connection, chat, rating and discovery services.
- The frontend is served on http://localhost:4200.
- Two verified test accounts (A and B), each logged in in a separate browser profile or incognito window, with an ACCEPTED connection and an open chat between them.
- DevTools is open on the Network tab in A's window.

1. In A's window click "Met Successfully" and confirm in the dialog.
   Expect: URL becomes `/rating/<connectionId>`; title "Rate your companion", no back arrow; B's name and avatar show; heading reads "How was your experience with <B's name>?".
2. Verify the photo.
   Expect: Network tab shows a GET to the photo URL under `.../user/...` with an `Authorization: Bearer ...` request header; the avatar `<img>` `src` starts with `blob:`; if B has no photo, initials show instead.
3. Verify the tag grid.
   Expect: five violet tags (Friendly, Respectful, On time, Good communication, Would travel again) and five red tags (Didn't show up, Fake destination, Abusive, Spam, Asked for money), in two columns; "Submit Rating" is disabled and there is NO "Skip" button.
4. Click "Friendly", then click it again.
   Expect: chip fills violet with white text and does a small pop; clicking again deselects it and Submit is disabled again.
5. Click "Abusive".
   Expect: chip fills red; "Negative reports may affect this user's account." appears; Submit becomes enabled.
6. In B's window, click "Met Successfully" and confirm so the chat closes for both.
   Expect: A's screen stays on `/rating/...` and is not re-navigated.
7. In A's window select "Friendly", then double-click "Submit Rating" quickly.
   Expect: exactly ONE `POST .../rating/ratings`; payload `{"connection_id":<n>,"rated_id":<B's id>,"tags":["Friendly"]}` (no rater id, no comment); response 201; toast "Thanks for rating your companion."; URL becomes `/radar`; browser Back does not return to the rating screen.
8. Retry-before-close: repeat steps 1-5 with a fresh connection, but B does NOT confirm. Select a tag and click Submit.
   Expect: 400 `CHAT_NOT_CLOSED`; inline red message "Your chat has not finished closing yet. Please wait a moment and tap Submit Rating again."; Submit stays enabled; once B confirms, clicking Submit again succeeds.
9. Timeout path: with a new connection, open a chat and let it auto-close by timeout (or shorten the chat-service auto-close timer for testing). A is auto-navigated to `/rating/<id>`.
   Expect: ghost "Skip" button under "Submit Rating"; Submit is disabled until a tag is selected.
10. Click "Skip".
    Expect: navigation to `/radar` with no POST in the Network tab; Back does not return to the rating screen.
11. Refresh on met path: repeat step 1, then press F5.
    Expect: same screen reloads with the companion shown and still no Skip.
12. Direct URL, timeout-closed chat: in a new tab open `http://localhost:4200/rating/<id of a timeout-closed chat>`.
    Expect: screen loads and Skip appears.
13. Direct URL, unknown connection: open `http://localhost:4200/rating/999999`.
    Expect: "This rating is not available." with a "Back to radar" button that goes to `/radar`.
14. Direct URL, met-closed chat: open `/rating/<id>` of a chat closed after Met Successfully in a new tab.
    Expect: no Skip button.
15. Already-rated: rate once, then open the same `/rating/<id>` URL and submit again.
    Expect: 409 shows toast "You have already rated this companion." and the app goes to `/radar`.
16. Duplicate push: after A rates and reaches `/radar`, have B finish the chat close.
    Expect: A stays on `/radar` and is not pulled back to the rating screen.
17. Photo failure: block the photo request in DevTools (right-click the request, "Block request URL") and reload.
    Expect: initials avatar shows and the rest of the screen works.
18. Leak check: watch the Console tab and address bar during steps 1-15.
    Expect: no logged errors from the rating screen; URL contains only the connection id (no tags, names or tokens).

**Cleanup:** log both accounts out (clears the in-memory `ratingResolved` set), close the incognito windows, and optionally `docker compose down`.
