## Manual QA — T070

**Prerequisites:**
- The full stack is running: `docker compose up` plus the gateway and user-service.
- The Angular dev server is running at http://localhost:4200.
- Two test accounts, both signed in with Google and onboarded:
  - A: gender Female with a photo.
  - B: gender Male.
- A third account C with gender Unspecified (onboarding incomplete) is optional, for the unlocked-gender case.
- Browser devtools are open on the Network tab.

1. Sign in as account A, open http://localhost:4200/radar, and click the person icon in the top bar.
   Expect: the URL becomes /profile. The Profile screen shows a violet gradient header, a 96px avatar, A's name, and a Verified badge if A is verified.
2. Look at the avatar and the Network tab.
   Expect: the avatar shows A's photo. If the photo is self-hosted, there is a GET to `.../user/uploads/photos/...` with an `Authorization: Bearer` header and a 200 response, and the img `src` starts with `blob:`. It is not the raw http URL. A Google-hosted photo loads with no Authorization header.
3. Look at the Gender row for A.
   Expect: a grey box with a lock icon reading "Female — cannot be changed". There are no gender pills.
4. Edit the Name field, then click Save.
   Expect: a PATCH to `/user/users/me` whose body contains only `name` (no `gender`). A "Profile saved" toast appears and the header name updates. Save is disabled while saving. Click Save twice quickly and confirm only one PATCH is sent.
5. Click the avatar, choose a non-image file (for example a .txt) if the picker allows it.
   Expect: the error text "Please choose an image file." and no request.
6. Click the avatar and choose a JPEG or PNG under the size limit.
   Expect: the avatar instantly shows a local preview and Save becomes enabled. Click Save. Expect a multipart POST to `/user/users/me/photo` with form field `photo` (Content-Type multipart/form-data, whole file, one request). The avatar then shows the new photo through the authenticated fetch.
7. Choose an oversized image, or one the backend rejects.
   Expect: the backend's error message in red under the form, with Save still usable. It must not contain a raw URL.
8. Click the gear icon in the app bar.
   Expect: the URL becomes /profile/settings. An Availability card, a Discovery card with "Women-only mode", and an Account card (name, email, phone, Identity, Member since) are shown.
9. In Availability, click the switch.
   Expect: the switch is disabled briefly. The PATCH body is `{"status":"looking"}` or `{"status":"unavailable"}`, whichever is the opposite of the current value. The switch and the pill ("Looking" or "Unavailable") move only after the 200 response.
10. With Settings open, in a second tab open /radar for the same account and start a scan. Then return to the Settings tab.
    Expect: on focus, a GET `/user/users/me` fires and the switch and pill change to Looking, with no manual reload. Then stop or exit the scan in the radar tab, return to Settings, and expect the switch to show Unavailable.
11. Click the women-only switch.
    Expect: the switch moves and a toast says "Women-only mode applies from your next scan". Open the Radar filters sheet and confirm its Women-only toggle shows the same value.
12. Sign out, sign in as account B (Male), and open /profile/settings.
    Expect: the Availability and Account cards appear, and no "Discovery" or "Women-only mode" section exists. Search the DOM for "Women-only" and confirm zero matches.
13. As B, open /profile.
    Expect: the gender row is locked and reads "Male — cannot be changed".
14. (Optional) Sign in as account C (Unspecified) and open /profile.
    Expect: gender pills are shown with the note about setting it once. Choose Female and click Save. The PATCH body contains `"gender":"female"` and the backend returns 200. After saving, the gender row becomes locked.
15. Failure case: stop user-service (or use devtools "Offline"), then reload /profile/settings.
    Expect: a red error message with a Retry button. After the service is back, click Retry and the screen loads. If you go offline only after Settings has loaded and then refocus the tab, the existing data stays visible with no error screen.
16. Switch to the Network tab's "Slow 3G" throttling and repeat step 9 while quickly switching to another tab and back.
    Expect: after the PATCH completes, the switch shows the value the server returned. It never snaps back to the old value.

**Cleanup:** Clear the throttling or offline setting in devtools. Set test accounts back to their prior status through the Settings toggle, and stop any scan left active. Close the extra tabs. Run `docker compose down` if you started the stack for this test.
